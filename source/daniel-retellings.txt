Two others stood on opposite banks of the river, and a question was put to the figure clothed in linen above the waters. How long would these wonders continue? With both hands raised towards heaven, he swore by the one who lives for ever and spoke of a time, times and half a time. The breaking of the holy people's power stood within the answer. Even this solemn disclosure did not turn the prophet into someone who understood every detail.~Daniel said plainly that he heard but did not understand, and asked what the outcome would be. The answer again directed him to go his way because the words were closed and sealed until the appointed time. Many would be purified and tested, while wicked people would continue in wickedness. The wise would understand. Further periods of one thousand two hundred and ninety days and one thousand three hundred and thirty-five days were mentioned in relation to the removal of the regular offering and the desolating abomination. Readers disagree over their detailed chronology. The chapter itself warns against pretending that the presence of numbers removes the need for humility about what has not been made plain.~The final word to Daniel was personal. He was to go his way until the end, rest and stand in his appointed place at the end of the days. The aged servant's future was held within God's promise even though questions remained. This ending gathers the whole book's call to faithfulness. Daniel had served through changing kingdoms, sought God in prayer and received visions that sometimes overwhelmed him. He did not need complete knowledge of the timetable to complete the life entrusted to him. Resurrection hope allowed him to leave the final outcome with the Lord. The reader is invited to the same patient trust, living wisely, helping others towards righteousness and resting confidence in the God whose faithful purpose reaches beyond death.|God's final promise includes resurrection and a secure place for his faithful servants. Unanswered questions need not prevent present obedience.|What responsibility can you fulfil faithfully today while leaving an unanswered question about the future with God?
