1:1-18|The Word Comes to Dwell Among Us|Receiving the One who makes the Father known|John opens before the first scene on earth. In the beginning the Word already was. The conflict between light and darkness is already present before John names the people who will see it.~A man named John was sent by God to bear witness to the light so that people might believe through him. He himself was a witness. The true light was coming into the world. The world was made through him, yet it failed to recognise him. He came to his own, and his own did not receive him. To those who did receive him and believe in his name, he gave the right to become children of God.~The Word became flesh and dwelt among us. John speaks as one who has seen his glory, full of grace and truth. The witness John cries out that the one coming after him ranks before him because he was before him.~From his fullness people have received grace. John names Moses and the law, then names Jesus Christ in connection with grace and truth. He ends the opening by speaking of the Son making the unseen God known. The person about whom witnesses will speak is already the one through whom the world came into being.|The opening puts God's initiative before any human response. Receiving Jesus begins with recognising who has come and what he makes known.|What does the opening tell you about the Word before it describes his coming into the world?
