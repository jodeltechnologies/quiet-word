Economic pressure was being answered through the use of the same gates that earlier chapters celebrated.~Marriages with women of Ashdod, Ammon and Moab raised further concern about covenant loyalty and children losing the community's language. Nehemiah confronted the men harshly, including physical treatment the narrative records without making it a model for pastoral care. He recalled Solomon's fall and expelled a priestly relative allied by marriage with Sanballat. The account concerns this community's covenant crisis and must not become a general licence for hostility across ethnic lines. Nehemiah restored priestly and Levite duties, wood offerings and firstfruits and ended by asking God to remember him for good. The book closes with renewed correction rather than a claim that everything was permanently settled. Rebuilding had brought real hope, while faithfulness still needed to be practised when public celebration and close supervision were no longer enough.|Good beginnings need continued obedience, fair oversight and correction.
