1:1-14|Gaius and the Travelling Brothers|Hospitality makes believers fellow workers in the truth|The elder writes to Gaius, whom he loves in the truth. He prays that Gaius may be well in body and that things may go well with him, just as his soul is doing well. Brothers have visited the elder and have spoken about the truth in Gaius and his habit of walking in it. Their report gives the elder deep joy. He says that he has no greater joy than hearing that his children walk in the truth. What he has heard comes from something the visitors experienced for themselves. Gaius has acted faithfully towards the brothers, including men who were strangers to him when they came. They have spoken of his love before the church, and now the elder asks him to send them on their way in a manner worthy of God.~The reason for that request concerns what the travellers are doing. They went out for the sake of the Name, taking nothing from the Gentiles. Their ability to go on depended in part on believers receiving and helping them. The elder says that people like these ought to be supported so that those who support them can become fellow workers with the truth. Gaius could take a real share in their work from his own home. Receiving people he had never met, seeing to their needs and helping them on the next part of the journey all mattered. The elder has put his encouragement to Gaius in writing because the brothers' reception elsewhere has become a point of trouble.~He had written something to the church, but Diotrephes loves to be first and refuses to receive the elder and his associates. If the elder comes, he will call attention to what Diotrephes is doing. Diotrephes spreads malicious words about them, refuses to welcome the brothers himself, stops those who wish to receive them, and puts people out of the church. A preference for being first has become a power over other people's doors and places in the congregation. The elder tells Gaius to imitate what is good. The person who does good is of God, while doing evil shows that someone has failed to see God. Gaius has a decision to make in a church where helping the brothers may come at a cost.~Demetrius receives a good report from everyone and from the truth itself, and the elder adds his own testimony. By the time Gaius reaches that commendation, he has heard what faithful conduct looks like in his own service and where it has broken down in Diotrephes. He now has another person whose conduct others can vouch for. The elder has more to say, but he hopes to see Gaius soon and speak with him face to face. He wishes him peace, conveys greetings from the friends and asks Gaius to greet the friends by name. The letter ends with the same personal care it showed at its opening, attending to individuals even as it addresses a serious matter in the church.|Believers share in the work of the truth when they give practical help to those serving Christ. The way we use influence can open that opportunity to others or close it against them.|Who is carrying out faithful work that you could help with practical support, and what would that help look like?
