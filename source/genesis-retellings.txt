1:1-2:25|A World Prepared for Life|God makes the heavens and the earth and gives humanity a place and a calling|Genesis opens with God creating the heavens and the earth. The earth is without form and empty, darkness lies over the deep, and God's Spirit moves over the waters. God speaks and light appears. He separates it from the darkness and names day and night. He makes an expanse between waters and then gathers the waters below so dry ground appears. The land brings forth seed-bearing plants and fruit trees according to their kinds. Lights in the heavens mark days, seasons and years, with the greater light ruling the day and the lesser ruling the night. Again and again God sees that what he has made is good. The account teaches the reader to look at the familiar world as something called into being and ordered by him.~God fills the waters with living creatures and the sky with birds, blesses them and calls them to multiply. The earth brings forth livestock, creeping things and wild animals. God makes humanity in his image, male and female, and blesses them. They are to multiply, fill the earth and rule over its living things. God assigns plants for food, and when he surveys everything he has made, it is very good. On the seventh day he rests from the work of creation, blesses the day and makes it holy. Human beings enter a world where work has a purpose and where the Creator has already set apart a day of rest.~The account turns to the man God formed from the dust of the ground. God breathes into him the breath of life and he becomes a living being. God plants a garden in Eden and places him there. Trees good for food and pleasing to see grow in it, including the tree of life and the tree of the knowledge of good and evil. A river waters the garden and divides into four named branches. The man is to work the garden and keep it. God gives him freedom to eat from its trees and warns him that eating from the tree of the knowledge of good and evil will bring death. The commission to care for the place comes with a limit that asks him to trust the one who gave it.~God sees that it is not good for the man to be alone. Animals and birds are brought to him and he names them, yet no suitable helper is found among them. God causes him to sleep, takes from his side and forms a woman. When she is brought to him, he recognises her as bone of his bones and flesh of his flesh. The account explains why a man leaves father and mother and is joined to his wife so they become one flesh. They are naked and unashamed. The chapters end in the garden with work to do, a word to keep and a relationship in which neither person needs to hide.|Human dignity comes from God's image. The world is his gift, and tending it, honouring his limits and cherishing the people alongside us are ways to answer his generosity.|What part of the life God has entrusted to you needs more faithful care this week?
