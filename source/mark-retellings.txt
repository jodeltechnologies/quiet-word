2:1-17|Through the Roof and Around the Table|Forgiveness and fellowship|When Jesus returned to Capernaum, word spread that he was in a house. To show his authority to forgive on earth, he told the man to rise, take up his bed and go home.~The man got up at once and went out in front of them all. The people were amazed and glorified God. Later Jesus went out beside the sea and taught there. Passing a tax booth, he saw Levi son of Alphaeus and called him to follow. Levi stood up and went with him.~At a meal, many tax collectors and sinners sat with Jesus and his disciples. The scribes and Pharisees questioned the disciples about his company. 4:26-41|A Growing Field and a Quiet Sea|Faith when you cannot control events|Jesus described seed growing while a farmer slept and a mustard seed becoming a large plant. 5:1-20|The Man Who Wanted to Go With Jesus|Witness starts at home|After the crossing, Jesus stepped out of the boat and a man came from among the tombs to meet him. The spirits begged Jesus to let them enter a herd of pigs feeding nearby. He allowed it. The herd rushed down the steep bank into the water and drowned. The herdsmen fled and told people in the town and countryside what had happened.~Those who came out saw the man sitting down, clothed and in his right mind. They also heard the account of what had happened to him and to the pigs. They were afraid and asked Jesus to leave their district. When Jesus got into the boat, the man who had been delivered asked to go with him.~Jesus gave him a different place to serve. He sent him home to his own people to tell them what the Lord had done for him and how he had shown him mercy. The man went through the Decapolis telling what Jesus had done. People marvelled at the report carried by the man who had once lived among the tombs.|The restored man received a task close to home. 5:21-43|Two Daughters in Need|Jesus receives the hidden sufferer|When Jesus crossed the lake again, a crowd gathered around him. The woman came trembling and told him the whole truth. He called her daughter and sent her away in peace.~While Jesus was still speaking, people arrived from Jairus's house to say the girl had died. They asked why he should trouble the teacher further. Jesus told Jairus not to be afraid, but to believe, and went on with Peter, James and John. At the house people were weeping loudly. When Jesus spoke of the child as sleeping, they laughed at him.~Jesus took the girl's parents and the three disciples into the room. Taking her by the hand, he told her to rise.
